# FixIt Forum

## Current state
I follow "Bathroom fan humming but not pulling air"; the August 30 replies say to check for a stuck damper flap at the duct connection, check the roof or soffit vent cap for a bird nest, and that if the blade spins slowly by hand the bearings are shot (a motor kit is about $30). [ep:gmail_153165f9ecc5c58b]
I follow "Bathroom exhaust fan not venting — attic duct question"; the August 15 replies say to pull sagging flex duct tight and keep the run short, use insulated duct, and check that the roof cap's damper flap isn't stuck. [ep:gmail_c487d936dbef7efc]
I follow "Basement window well filling with water after heavy rain"; the August 10 replies say to dig out the clogged drain at the bottom of the well, add a clear polycarbonate cover, and make sure the gutters aren't dumping next to the well. [ep:gmail_359229725454a90f]
I follow "Bathroom fan runs but barely pulls air: duct issue?"; the June 20 replies say to check for a damper flap stuck shut at the roof or soffit cap and to swap sagging flex duct for rigid. [ep:gmail_fe99e7eee7d6006a]
